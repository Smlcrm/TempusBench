# TempusBench Provenance Tables (R1a, R1b, R1c)

Provenance of all 70 TempusBench forecasting tasks. Descriptions are taken from the Task Explorer on the live leaderboard (https://benchmark.smlcrm.com/#task-explorer). Bracketed citation numbers refer to the reference list of the TempusBench paper (NeurIPS 2026 submission 3210); "Appendix D.x" marks synthetic tasks whose generating processes are specified in closed form in the paper.
